## 📌 Overview

Revora is an AI-driven growth and agentic commerce platform designed to help merchants increase revenue while keeping every money-related action explainable, bounded, and verifiable.

Instead of acting as a simple product search interface, Revora follows an agent-style workflow:

```text
Customer Intent
      ↓
Intent Detection
      ↓
Product Matching & Ranking
      ↓
Cross-Sell Opportunities
      ↓
Explainable Recommendation
      ↓
Customer Confirmation
      ↓
Razorpay Checkout
      ↓
Payment Verification
      ↓
Merchant Analytics & Audit Trail
